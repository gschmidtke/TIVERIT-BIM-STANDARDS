# TIVERIT BIM Standards — Framework at a Glance

**An open, machine-readable framework for organizing standards—not a requirement to publish your standards.**

A document library stores standards. A governed standards system lets people, AI, and software **retrieve the applicable rule, identify its authority, and determine how the result can be verified**.

## How TIVERIT sorts information

| Coordinate | Question | Scheme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Domain** | What is this about? | **S1–S9:** governance; information requirements; production environment; naming and identity; coordination; federation and linking; delivery; parameters and classification; tools and automation. Multiple domains may apply. |
| **Layer** | Where does it sit in the governance stack? | **L1–L7:** external authority; doctrine; architecture; normative standard; workflow; instruction; configuration or machine payload. |
| **Content type** | What does this object do? | **Standards constrain. Workflows coordinate. Instructions execute. Processes explain.** Registries, configurations and conformance tests have distinct supporting roles. |
| **Authority and lifecycle** | Can it be relied on? | Stable identifier, source/provenance, version, status, governing authority, relationships and evidence requirements, as applicable. |

One source document may mix multiple types. Its meaning is classified into related objects rather than duplicating rules or treating an explanation as enforceable policy.

## What happens when someone asks a question?

**Question → identify subject and governing class → retrieve applicable rule and project context → return authority, conditions, version and verification path.**

Missing facts and conflicts are surfaced, not invented. Tools may execute supported instructions, but successful execution alone does not establish compliance or approval.

## The framework and the content are different assets

The **framework** is publicly inspectable and reusable. A company's **content**—its standards, proprietary methods, configurations, contracts and project records—can remain in its own controlled private environment. Appropriate permissions and contractual safeguards must be established by the adopting organization; the open framework does not automatically secure private information.

TIVERIT's commercial services include standards assessment, organization, company-specific derivation, integration, automation and ongoing governance.

**Open framework. Private or public content under its owner's control. Commercial implementation expertise.**
