## 3. Canonical write and promotion authority

This repository's public licensing terms do not create canonical write access.

External contributors — human or AI — submit proposals through the governed Inbox route described in `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Inbox material is non-authoritative. Only curator-reviewed, normalized, provenance-checked, validated, and explicitly promoted material may enter canonical areas.

A fork, adaptation, redistribution, or downstream implementation may use the licensed material but is not the canonical TIVERIT BIM Standards repository and must not represent itself as having TIVERIT approval or authority merely because it derives from this repository.

## 5. Third-party and external standards material

The licenses above apply only to material for which TIVERIT or the relevant contributor has the rights necessary to grant the stated permissions.

They do **not** relicense third-party standards, publications, figures, tables, definitions, client documents, proprietary project information, or other externally copyrighted or licensed source material merely because that material is cited, identified, mapped, referenced, or used as evidence by TIVERIT.

External standards such as ISO, EN, BS, or other publisher-controlled publications remain subject to the rights and license terms of their respective rights holders. The public repository should normally retain only lawful identifiers, provenance, applicability metadata, mappings, citations, independently authored TIVERIT rules, and other material permitted by the repository's external-standards governance.

## 6. Private source-evidence repositories

TIVERIT may use separately controlled private repositories or other authorized source stores to hold source evidence used during curator verification. Access to such a source does not imply permission to redistribute it.

The governing distinction is:

**source access ≠ redistribution rights ≠ canonical authority**

Material from a private evidence source may be promoted into this public repository only to the extent that TIVERIT has the right to publish that material. Otherwise the public repository records provenance, identifiers, mappings, independently authored normalization, or other lawful derived governance without publishing the protected source text.

## 7. Attribution and provenance

Downstream users should preserve repository and object provenance sufficient to distinguish TIVERIT-authored governance from external authority and third-party source material. Where CC BY 4.0 applies, attribution must satisfy that license.

## 8. No implied external-standard compliance

Use of TIVERIT material, or a TIVERIT mapping to an external standard, does not by itself establish compliance or conformity with that external standard. Applicability, authority, evidence, and conformance remain governed by the relevant TIVERIT objects, project instruments, and external-standard terms.

## 9. Scope markings control

Where a specific file, object, source, or directory carries more specific rights or provenance metadata, that specific marking controls for that material. If rights are unknown or unresolved, the material must not be assumed to be covered by the repository-wide license grant.
